Sailorlyf Bridge

Learn. Recall. Practise. Build confidence with maritime rules and buoyage.

Sailorlyf Bridge is a mobile maritime learning and revision app designed for seafarers, cadets, and maritime students. It combines structured reference material with learner explanations, quizzes, recall practice, scenarios, notes, and progress tools.

V1 Learning Modules

  • COLREG — browse and read the official rule text in English, supported by learner explanations, search, bookmarks, Rule Reader tools, notes, audio/read-aloud controls, Study by Rule, Sound Signals, Exam Tips, scenarios, Recall, Hard Recall, and COLREG quizzes.
  • IALA Buoyage System — learn buoyage marks for Regions A and B, including mark meanings, light characteristics, ECDIS-related learning content, and an IALA quiz.

Free and Premium

Sailorlyf Bridge includes both Free and Premium learning access.

  • Official COLREG rule text remains available without Premium.
  • IALA learning content and the IALA quiz remain available without Premium.
  • Selected COLREG explanations, quizzes, recall exercises, and scenarios are available as Free previews.
  • Sailorlyf Premium unlocks additional V1 COLREG learning content through monthly or annual subscriptions where available.

Languages

The app supports learner-facing interface and educational content in English, Filipino/Tagalog, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Indonesian, and Spanish where available. Official COLREG rule text remains in English.

Accounts and Progress

Users can sign in using supported account providers, including Google and Sign in with Apple on supported devices. Signed-in users can use account-linked learning features such as cloud-backed bookmarks, progress, last-opened state, highlights, and notes where available.

Educational Use

Important: Sailorlyf Bridge is an educational and study-support tool. It does not replace official COLREG text, official IALA publications, certified maritime training, vessel procedures, voyage-specific information, navigational equipment, or professional maritime judgment.
