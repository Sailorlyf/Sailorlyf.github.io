Sailorlyf Support

Sailorlyf is a maritime learning platform focused on helping seafarers, cadets, and maritime students learn and revise essential maritime topics, with launch modules covering COLREG and the IALA Buoyage System.

Support Email

For any question, issue, or feedback, contact us at:
sailorlyf@gmail.com

We aim to respond as soon as reasonably possible.

Subscription Help

As subscription features are introduced, billing for Sailorlyf subscriptions will be handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. If you have a question about a charge, renewal, or refund, please first check your purchase history in the relevant store. For anything else, email us and include the approximate date of the transaction and the store you purchased from.

Restore Purchases

If you have purchased a subscription and it is not showing in the app, you will be able to use the Restore Purchases option in the app once subscription features are available. If restoring does not work, make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase, then contact us at sailorlyf@gmail.com.

Account & Login Help

If account creation becomes available and you have trouble signing in, try resetting your password from the sign-in screen. If the problem persists, email us with the email address on the account and a description of the issue. If you would like your account removed instead, see the Delete Account page.
