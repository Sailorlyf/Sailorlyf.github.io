Sailorlyf Bridge Support

Sailorlyf Bridge is a maritime learning and study-support app covering COLREG and the IALA Maritime Buoyage System, with quizzes, recall exercises, scenarios, sound-signal learning, notes, bookmarks, progress tracking, and other study tools.

Support Email

For questions, technical issues, subscription-access problems, account help, or feedback, contact:
sailorlyf@gmail.com

We aim to respond as soon as reasonably possible.

Sign-In Help

Sailorlyf Bridge supports Google Sign-In and, on supported Apple devices, Sign in with Apple. If sign-in fails, check your internet connection and confirm that you can access the Google account or Apple Account you are using. Sailorlyf does not manage the password for those external accounts.

Premium Subscription Help

Premium subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by the store through which you purchased.

Deleting the app or your Sailorlyf account does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased Premium and access is not showing, use Restore Purchases in the app. Make sure you are using the same Apple App Store or Google Play account associated with the original purchase.

Premium access may require periodic online verification. If you have been offline for an extended period, reconnect to the internet and reopen or refresh the app before trying Restore Purchases.

Offline Use

Many learning features can be used without a continuous internet connection after content is available on the device. Account synchronization, sign-in, purchase restoration, and Premium entitlement verification require connectivity at appropriate times.

Languages

Sailorlyf Bridge provides learner-facing interface and educational content in English, Filipino/Tagalog, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Indonesian, and Spanish where available. Official COLREG rule text remains in English.

Account Deletion

You can initiate account deletion from within Sailorlyf Bridge when signed in. See the Delete Account page for what is deleted and important subscription information.

Before Contacting Support

  • Check that you are using the latest available version of Sailorlyf Bridge.
  • Restart the app if a screen or entitlement does not refresh correctly.
  • Reconnect to the internet for sign-in, cloud synchronization, or subscription verification issues.
  • Use Restore Purchases if a valid subscription is not reflected in the app.
