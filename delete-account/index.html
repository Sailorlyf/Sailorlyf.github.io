Delete Your Sailorlyf Bridge Account

If you have a Sailorlyf account, you can initiate permanent account deletion directly from within the Sailorlyf Bridge app while signed in.

Important: Deleting your Sailorlyf account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Cancel the subscription separately through the store if you do not want it to renew.

How to Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open Sailorlyf Bridge and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to the account section and choose Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion information and confirm the request.

If you cannot access the app or your account, contact sailorlyf@gmail.com for assistance.

What Is Deleted

Account deletion is intended to remove Sailorlyf-controlled account information and cloud learning data associated with your account, including:

  • your Sailorlyf account/profile record;
  • bookmarks;
  • progress or completion state;
  • last-opened learning state;
  • highlights; and
  • notes.

Information may be retained where required or permitted by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.

External Sign-In Accounts

Deleting your Sailorlyf account does not delete or close your Google account or Apple Account. Those external accounts remain controlled by Google or Apple.

Local Data

Some preferences or cached learning information may remain locally on a device until the app data is cleared or the app is removed. Deleting the app from your device by itself does not delete a Sailorlyf account stored on our backend.

Cancel a Subscription

To stop future subscription charges, cancel directly through the store used for the purchase:

  • Apple App Store: Open Settings on your Apple device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select Sailorlyf Bridge, and cancel if the option is available.
  • Google Play: Open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, select Sailorlyf Bridge, and cancel.

Need Help?

For account-deletion questions or if you cannot complete deletion in the app, email sailorlyf@gmail.com.
