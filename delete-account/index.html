Delete Your Sailorlyf Account

This page explains how account deletion will work for Sailorlyf. If account creation ships in V1, a final in-app deletion path will be added before launch. Until then, deletion requests are handled by email.

Important: Deleting your Sailorlyf account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Subscriptions must be managed separately through the relevant store.

What Happens When You Delete

When you request deletion of your Sailorlyf account:

  • Your account and profile data will be removed.
  • Synced learning data (progress, bookmarks, and study activity stored in association with your account) will be deleted.
  • Deletion is subject to any retention we are legally required to maintain.

Deleting the app from your device does not delete your account.

How to Request Deletion

Email your deletion request to:

sailorlyf@gmail.com

Suggested subject line: Sailorlyf Account Deletion Request

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Sailorlyf account where possible, so we can verify and process it.

Cancel a Subscription

To stop future subscription charges, cancel directly through the store you used:

  • Apple App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, then select and cancel the subscription.
  • Google Play: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, then select and cancel the subscription.
