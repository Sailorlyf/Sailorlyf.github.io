Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. About Sailorlyf Bridge

Sailorlyf Bridge is a maritime learning and study-support application for seafarers, cadets, and maritime students. It includes learning and revision tools for topics such as COLREG and the IALA Maritime Buoyage System.

2. Information We Collect

The information we process depends on how you use the app.

  • Account information — if you sign in, we may receive identifiers and profile information such as your email address, display name, provider identifier, and authentication metadata from Google or Apple.
  • Learning data — account-linked learning information may include bookmarks, progress or completion state, last-opened position, highlights, notes, and similar study data.
  • App preferences — settings such as selected language, audio or reading preferences, and other app configuration may be stored locally on your device and, where supported, associated with your account.
  • Subscription information — limited purchase and entitlement information such as product, subscription status, renewal or expiry state, and entitlement status may be processed to provide Premium access.
  • Technical information — limited technical information may be processed by service providers as necessary to operate, secure, diagnose, and support the app.

3. Authentication

Sailorlyf Bridge supports Google Sign-In and, on supported Apple devices, Sign in with Apple. Authentication is handled through these providers and our authentication backend. Apple users may choose Apple's private email relay feature, in which case we may receive a relay email address instead of the user's personal email address.

4. Learning Data and Cloud Sync

Some learning data is stored locally on your device. When you are signed in, eligible learning data may also be stored in association with your Sailorlyf account so it can be restored or synchronized. This may include bookmarks, progress, last-opened state, highlights, and notes.

We do not sell your learning data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

5. Subscriptions and Payment Information

Premium subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Sailorlyf does not directly receive or store your full card number, CVV, bank-account credentials, or raw UPI credentials.

We use RevenueCat to help manage subscription entitlements. RevenueCat may process app-user identifiers, store transaction information, product information, subscription status, and entitlement information required to determine whether Premium access is active.

6. How We Use Information

We use information described in this policy to:

  • authenticate users and maintain account sessions;
  • operate Sailorlyf Bridge and provide learning features;
  • save, synchronize, and restore eligible learning data;
  • determine Free and Premium access;
  • restore purchases and troubleshoot subscription access;
  • protect the app and related services from misuse;
  • respond to support requests; and
  • process account deletion requests.

7. Service Providers

We use service providers that process information only as needed to provide their services, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, account services, and account-linked cloud learning data.
  • Google — Google Sign-In where used.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store distribution, and Apple in-app purchases where used.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management.
  • Google Play — Android app distribution and Google Play purchases where used.

These providers may process data under their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Advertising and Tracking

Sailorlyf Bridge does not display third-party advertising. We do not sell personal information. We do not use your learning data for third-party advertising.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. When an account is deleted, Sailorlyf-controlled account data and associated cloud learning data are deleted or de-identified, except where retention is required or permitted by law.

10. Account and Data Deletion

You can initiate deletion of your Sailorlyf account from within the app when signed in. Additional instructions are available on our Delete Account page.

Account deletion removes Sailorlyf-controlled account/profile information and associated cloud learning data, subject to legally required or permitted retention. Deleting your Sailorlyf account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play.

Deleting your Sailorlyf account does not delete or close your Google account or Apple Account.

11. Children's Privacy

Sailorlyf Bridge is intended for maritime learners and is not directed to children under the age at which they can legally consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address the request.

12. International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, those providers are responsible for using appropriate safeguards for international data transfers under the laws that apply to them.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information, including access controls and authenticated access to account-linked data. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Your Choices

You may use available app features without signing in where the app permits. You may sign out at any time, manage subscriptions through the applicable app store, or delete your Sailorlyf account using the available deletion process.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Sailorlyf Bridge, our service providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the "Last updated" date when revisions are published.

16. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at sailorlyf@gmail.com.
