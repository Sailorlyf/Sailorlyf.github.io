Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Note: This policy describes the intended data practices for Sailorlyf as account and subscription features are introduced. Final production data practices may evolve before launch, and this policy will be updated to reflect them.

1. About Sailorlyf

Sailorlyf is a maritime learning platform for seafarers, cadets, and maritime students. The app provides structured learning and revision tools for maritime topics such as COLREG and the IALA Buoyage System.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Sailorlyf may collect:

  • Account information — such as your email address, if account creation is available and you choose to create an account.
  • Learning data — your progress within learning modules, such as topics studied, bookmarks, and activity history, particularly if you enable optional cloud progress sync.
  • Device and diagnostic information — basic technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the app.

3. Payment Information

If subscriptions become available, all payments will be processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Sailorlyf does not directly store card numbers, CVV, bank credentials, or raw UPI credentials. We may receive limited, non-sensitive entitlement information (such as whether you hold an active subscription) from our subscription management provider.

4. How Information Is Used

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate and maintain the app
  • Sync and restore your learning progress, if you opt in
  • Manage subscription entitlements, if subscriptions are offered
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests
  • Process account deletion requests

5. Third-Party Services

Sailorlyf plans to rely on the following services as account and subscription features are introduced:

  • Supabase — for account/authentication services and optional cloud progress storage.
  • RevenueCat — for subscription entitlement management.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play — for app distribution and payment processing.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

6. Learning Data

Your learning data (progress, bookmarks, and study activity) is stored locally on your device. If cloud progress sync is introduced and enabled, learning data may be stored in association with your account so it can be restored across devices. We do not sell learning data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or comply with legal obligations. Data associated with a deleted account is removed or de-identified, subject to any legally required retention.

8. Account and Data Deletion

If account creation ships, you will be able to request deletion of your Sailorlyf account. Deletion removes your account/profile data and synced learning data, subject to legally required retention. Please see our Delete Account page for instructions. Note that deleting your Sailorlyf account does not automatically cancel any subscription; see below.

9. Children's Privacy

Sailorlyf is intended for maritime learners and is not directed at children under the age at which they can consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as features evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date on this page, and where appropriate, within the app.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at sailorlyf@gmail.com.
